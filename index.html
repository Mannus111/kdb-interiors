Studio

Spaces crafted
with intention

KDB Interiors is founded on the belief that spaces should be felt before they are designed. Through thoughtful understanding of how people live, move, and experience their environments, each interior is shaped with purpose and authenticity. From tailored residential homes to dynamic commercial environments and curated hospitality experiences, KDB brings a refined eye and thoughtful process to each commission.

Rooted in a contemporary yet timeless approach, the studio creates interiors that feel considered, layered, and deeply connected to the people who experience them. Each space is shaped through thoughtful materiality, honest detailing, and a balance between functionality and atmosphere.
